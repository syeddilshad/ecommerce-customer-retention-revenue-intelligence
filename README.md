# E-Commerce Customer Retention & Revenue Intelligence

**An end-to-end Data Analytics project using PostgreSQL, SQL, Power BI, and DAX to analyze revenue performance, customer purchasing behavior, retention, returns, and customer lifetime value.**

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a synthetic but realistic e-commerce dataset covering January 2023 to December 2025. It transforms transactional data into business insights through SQL-based analysis and interactive Power BI dashboards.

The analysis focuses on revenue growth, order performance, customer retention, product-category contribution, return behavior, and customer segmentation. The objective is to demonstrate how data analytics can help businesses understand performance, identify customer risks, and make more informed decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze monthly and yearly revenue and order trends.
- Identify the product categories contributing most to delivered revenue.
- Compare revenue generated by new and returning customers.
- Measure repeat customer behavior and purchasing frequency.
- Identify at-risk and lost customer segments.
- Analyze customer lifetime value and RFM segments.
- Evaluate order statuses, return rates, and return reasons.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

## Dataset Overview

The project uses a synthetic e-commerce dataset designed to represent a realistic retail business scenario.

| Table | Records | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| Customers | 12,000 | Customer profiles, demographics, and signup information |
| Orders | 75,000 | Order transactions, values, dates, and statuses |
| Products | 500 | Product details, categories, prices, and costs |
| Returns | 7,034 | Return records, reasons, and refund information |

**Coverage:** January 2023 – December 2025

*Note: The dataset is synthetic and is used for portfolio demonstration. The findings describe this dataset, not the performance of a real company.*

## Analytical Workflow

1. **Data validation:** Checked data quality, duplicate records, missing values, invalid values, and order-value consistency.
2. **SQL analysis:** Queried transaction data to investigate revenue trends, category performance, customer behavior, and returns.
3. **Customer analytics:** Analyzed purchase frequency, repeat customers, RFM segments, customer value, and retention-related indicators.
4. **KPI development:** Defined business metrics for revenue, delivered orders, customer behavior, and returns.
5. **Dashboard development:** Built a two-page Power BI report for executive performance monitoring and customer intelligence.
6. **Business interpretation:** Summarized key findings and developed data-informed recommendations.

## Key Business Findings

### 1. Revenue Growth

Delivered revenue increased from **₹10.82 crore in 2023** to **₹93.27 crore in 2025**.

**Business interpretation:** The dataset shows a substantial increase in delivered revenue across the analyzed years. Comparing order volume, average order value, and comparable reporting periods helps assess the factors associated with this growth.

### 2. Order Volume and Average Order Value

Delivered orders increased from **4,737 in 2023** to **40,608 in 2025**, while average order value remained close to **₹23K**.

**Business interpretation:** The increase in delivered order volume, alongside a relatively stable average order value, is consistent with volume being a major contributor to revenue growth.

### 3. Category Contribution

Electronics accounted for approximately **67% of delivered revenue**.

**Business interpretation:** Electronics is the leading revenue-contributing category in this dataset. Its performance and return-related financial impact warrant particular attention.

### 4. Returning Customer Contribution

Returning customers contributed approximately **82.7% of delivered revenue**, while the repeat customer rate was approximately **88.03%**.

**Business interpretation:** Returning customers account for a substantial share of delivered revenue. Understanding their purchase frequency and value can help guide customer loyalty and retention strategies.

*The repeat customer rate and returning-customer revenue share measure different things and should be interpreted separately.*

### 5. Order Status and Returns

| Order Status | Share of Orders |
|---|---:|
| Delivered | 84.59% |
| Returned | 9.38% |
| Cancelled | 6.04% |

Fashion had the highest category return rate at approximately **12.73%**, while Electronics had the largest financial impact from returns due to its higher order values.

**Business interpretation:** Category-specific return analysis can help prioritize investigations into product information, customer expectations, quality, and delivery-related issues.

*Percentages may not sum to exactly 100% because of rounding.*

### 6. Customer Intelligence

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Repeat Customer Rate | 88.03% |
| At-Risk Customers | 1,694 |
| Average Customer Lifetime Value | ₹132.57K |
| High-Value Customers | 236 |

**Business interpretation:** The customer analysis identifies segments that can be investigated for retention opportunities and differentiated engagement. The at-risk classification depends on the project's RFM segmentation rules.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Strengthen Customer Retention
Investigate at-risk and lost customer segments and test targeted re-engagement campaigns. Measure the results using subsequent purchase rates and customer revenue.

### 2. Protect High-Value Customers
Evaluate loyalty benefits and personalized engagement for high-value customers. Track repeat purchases and customer value to assess effectiveness.

### 3. Investigate Fashion Returns
Review return reasons and investigate whether sizing information, product descriptions, images, quality, or delivery issues contribute to Fashion returns.

### 4. Monitor Electronics Performance
Monitor Electronics revenue alongside return rates, refund amounts, and return reasons to understand its overall financial contribution and potential risks.

### 5. Encourage Repeat Purchases
Analyze purchasing intervals and frequency to identify opportunities for relevant cross-selling, replenishment reminders, and repeat-purchase campaigns.

*These recommendations are proposed actions based on the observed patterns; their effectiveness would need to be tested using business outcomes.*
